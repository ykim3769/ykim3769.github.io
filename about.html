Biography

Yong-Guk Kim is a Professor of Computer Engineering at Sejong University, Seoul. He received his Ph.D. from the University of Cambridge and held research positions in the Netherlands and the US before joining Sejong. His research spans facial expression generation, anomaly detection, autonomous drones, and multimodal LLMs for HCI/HRI.
