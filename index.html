Professor · Computer Engineering

Yong-Guk Kim

Department of Computer Engineering, Sejong University

Seoul, Republic of Korea · Ph.D., University of Cambridge

His research has focused on the intersection of vision, generation and interaction: facial expression recognition and generation, anomaly detection, autonomous drones, and multimodal large language models for human–computer and human–robot interaction.

Email Google Scholar ORCID DBLP

Research Areas

Four connected directions, from perceiving people and scenes to machines that respond to them.

Facial Expression Recognition & Generation

Facial feature representation, face tracking, 3D gaze and hand-pose estimation, drowsiness detection, and instruction-driven 3D facial expression generation.

10 papers →

Anomaly Detection

Video anomaly detection in aerial and ground-based footage with residual autoencoders and spatio-temporal transformers, and GAN-based detection of attacks on mobile devices.

3 papers →

Autonomous Drones

Deep reinforcement learning for obstacle avoidance and navigation, drone simulators, human–drone interfaces, and generative-AI-driven UAV autonomy.

8 papers →

Multimodal LLMs for HCI / HRI

Language grounding for 3D navigation, LLM-based meme understanding, UI/UX for generative AI, and LLM–RL coupling for immersive 3D interaction.

5 papers →
